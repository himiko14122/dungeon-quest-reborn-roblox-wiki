## Attack Patterns and Phase Breakdown

The Frost Warden cycles through three distinct combat phases, each introducing new attack patterns that compound in complexity as the fight progresses and the boss loses health. Recognizing these phase transitions before they occur provides critical advantages in damage optimization and survivability, allowing experienced players to plan their damage windows in advance rather than reacting blindly to every attack.


### Phase One: Opening Assault

The fight begins with a predictable three-hit melee combo consisting of horizontal slash, overhead strike, and a sweeping finish that knocks players back if it connects fully. This opening sequence repeats every eight to ten seconds during the first phase, establishing a rhythm that players can exploit by attacking during the recovery frames between combinations. The horizontal slash covers approximately 120 degrees in front of the boss, requiring immediate perpendicular movement rather than backward retreat to avoid effectively.

Experienced players identify the overhead strike by the characteristic glow that appears on the Frost Warden's weapon approximately half a second before execution, providing sufficient reaction time for players maintaining appropriate distance. The sweeping third hit presents the greatest threat to melee-focused characters who must commit to damage positions while simultaneously preparing to dodge the follow-up. **Positioning at the boss's flank rather than directly in front eliminates the need to predict which direction the horizontal slash will travel**, simplifying the dodge sequence considerably.

Frost projectiles begin appearing in phase one as the boss transitions toward mid-health, launched in three-round bursts toward the player's current position with slight tracking capability that requires active strafing to avoid completely. These projectiles deal moderate frost damage and apply a brief slowing debuff that compounds with other frost effects throughout the encounter, making early mitigation particularly valuable.


### Phase Two: Frost Expansion

Below fifty percent health, the Frost Warden enters an aggressive second phase characterized by expanded attack options and reduced recovery windows between combinations. The melee combo sequence gains a fourth hit consisting of a ground slam that creates frost nova damage in a moderate radius around the boss, punishing players who cluster too closely during damage windows. This phase transition typically catches unaware players who developed comfortable rhythms during phase one, resulting in sudden unexpected damage that depletes accumulated health.

The frost nova ground slam telegraph occurs through a one-second蓄力 animation where the boss raises both arms skyward, creating a visible frost aura that expands outward before the actual damage registers. **This extended telegraph provides a reliable window for characters with mobility spells to reposition completely**, but players relying on base movement speed must begin dodging immediately upon recognizing the animation rather than waiting for visual confirmation. Mistiming this dodge results in damage plus the slowing effect, creating dangerous stacking that reduces survivability throughout the remainder of the phase.

Frost pillars begin erupting from the ground during phase two, targeting player positions with delayed strike timing that requires continuous movement rather than stationary damage dealing. These pillars create terrain hazards that persist for several seconds, gradually reducing the usable arena space as the fight progresses. Smart players track pillar locations mentally to avoid corner themselves, while careless players frequently find themselves trapped against arena boundaries with no escape routes from subsequent attacks.


### Phase Three: Final Stand

The third and final phase triggers below twenty-five percent health, transforming the Frost Warden into an absolute offensive machine with minimal recovery periods between devastating attack strings. The boss gains access to a sweeping frost wave attack that travels across the entire arena width, requiring players to position themselves behind cover objects or time precise jumps to avoid taking heavy damage. This attack pattern represents the primary cause of failed kills during the final phase, catching players focused purely on damage output off-guard.

During this desperate final stand, the Frost Warden enters an enrage timer that progressively shortens attack recovery windows, demanding increasingly aggressive damage strategies from players who wish to complete the kill before accumulating too many deaths. The enrage mechanic doesn't instantly kill players but rather creates unsustainable damage pressure that eventually overwhelms any defensive build. Coordinating burst damage windows during this phase separates successful kills from frustrating failures.

The phase three transition also introduces overlapping attack patterns where multiple telegraphed abilities execute simultaneously, creating genuine chaos for players unfamiliar with the rhythm. **Understanding which attacks take priority during overlap situations—typically the ground slam frost nova due to its large radius—allows players to formulate escape plans rather than panicking through random movement**. Practice and repetition remain the only reliable methods for internalizing these complex overlapping sequences.
