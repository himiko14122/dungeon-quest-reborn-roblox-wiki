Novice players frequently make predictable errors regarding **weapon upgrade cost** management that significantly slow their progression and create frustrating resource shortages. Players also commonly neglect the opportunity cost analysis, failing to consider what other improvements their gold could fund before committing to expensive late-tier upgrades.

Upgrading before attempting harder content makes sense when current weapons struggle with clear speed or survival, as the damage increase accelerates runs enough to offset delayed upgrade costs. **Do different weapon types have different upgrade cost multipliers?**

All weapons follow the same percentage-based upgrade cost scaling, though absolute costs vary based on base weapon stats and rarity classification. The upgrade cost formula applies uniformly across weapon categories including daggers, swords, axes, staves, and hammers. For instance, upgrading a Common-tier Iron Dagger from +0 to +1 costs significantly less than upgrading a Legendary Dragon Slayer Sword to the same level, due to the base damage multiplier and rarity multiplier applied to the percentage formula.

**How do I calculate if an upgrade is worth the cost?**

Divide the gold cost by the resulting damage increase percentage to determine cost efficiency, then compare this ratio against your other upgrade options and equipment alternatives.
