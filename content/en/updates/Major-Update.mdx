## Understanding Major Update vs Minor Update Releases

The distinction between a Dungeon Quest Reborn major update and a minor update determines how drastically the game changes and how much preparation you need. Major updates typically introduce entirely new content systems, additional dungeons, fresh character classes, or complete reworks of existing mechanics. These releases often reset the meta entirely, meaning weapons and equipment that dominated the previous version may become obsolete or significantly weaker.

Minor updates, by contrast, focus on refinement rather than transformation. A Dungeon Quest Reborn minor update might include bug fixes, small balance adjustments, quality-of-life improvements, or limited seasonal content additions. These updates rarely require you to completely rebuild your character but instead offer incremental improvements to your existing setup. Community reports from players indicate that minor updates tend to arrive more frequently—sometimes every one to two weeks—while major updates typically follow longer development cycles spanning several months.
