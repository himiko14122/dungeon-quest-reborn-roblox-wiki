The new boss in Dungeon Quest Reborn operates on a three-phase system that escalates in difficulty as the encounter progresses, and each phase demands different weapon and spell choices to survive the escalating damage output. Phase one features a sweeping melee attack that covers a wide arc in front of the boss, dealing heavy damage to warriors who charge in without spacing properly, while the boss also fires homing projectiles that require lateral dodges rather than backward movement to evade cleanly. Phase two activates an enrage mechanic that doubles the boss attack speed after the health bar drops below sixty percent, and players who fail to adjust their dodge timing during this transition frequently experience one-shot kills even with decent armor ratings.

Phase three introduces a summon mechanic where the boss spawns two adds that channel energy into a devastating area-of-effect blast if they are not interrupted within five seconds of appearing, making crowd-control spells or high-burst weapons essential for maintaining control of the battlefield. Players report that positioning the party at the boss's flank during phase three dramatically reduces the effectiveness of the sweeping attack and creates windows for uninterrupted damage output between dodging sequences. The boss enrage timer activates at the three-minute mark if the encounter is not concluded, triggering an exponential damage increase that makes survival effectively impossible for all but the most overgeared groups.
